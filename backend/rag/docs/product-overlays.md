# Product Eligibility and Overlays

Overlays are lender-specific rules that sit on top of agency or investor
guidelines. They affect eligibility and pricing adjustments, not the base rate
itself.

## Conventional

- Minimum credit score 620 for standard conventional loans.
- Maximum loan-to-value 97% for qualifying first-time buyers, otherwise 95%.
- Private mortgage insurance is required above 80% loan-to-value.
- Investment properties carry an additional pricing adjustment and a lower
  maximum loan-to-value.

## FHA

- Minimum credit score 580 for the standard low-down-payment option.
- A credit score overlay applies below 580: manual underwriting and
  compensating factors are required, and some programs are unavailable.
- Upfront and annual mortgage insurance premiums apply regardless of
  loan-to-value.
- The property must be the borrower's primary residence.

## VA

- Available to eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and certain
  surviving spouses with a valid Certificate of Eligibility.
- No down payment is required up to the county loan limit.
- No monthly mortgage insurance; a one-time funding fee applies unless the
  borrower is exempt.

## Occupancy and property type

Primary residences receive the most favourable overlays. Second homes and
investment properties tighten eligibility and add pricing adjustments. Condos and
manufactured homes have additional project and property approval requirements.
